About RETINA AI

What is diabetic retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy is a complication of diabetes that affects blood vessels in the retina. Eye-care professionals use retinal examination to assess it.

Model and dataset

This academic prototype uses a PyTorch EfficientNet-B0 classifier trained on the APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection dataset. It outputs one of five grades: 0 No DR, 1 Mild, 2 Moderate, 3 Severe, and 4 Proliferative DR.

System architecture

Browser upload → FastAPI → experimental image quality assessment → CLAHE image enhancement → EfficientNet-B0 prediction → Grad-CAM explanation → browser results and report.

Current limitation: The model predicts from the original image using its training transforms, not the displayed enhanced image. The quality checker does not yet reliably reject ungradable fundus images before classification. Grad-CAM is available for new analyses when returned by the backend; its highlighted areas are not a clinical diagnosis.

Important safety note

This system has not been clinically validated. Its confidence score is a model output, not a measure of diagnostic certainty. Do not use these results for medical decisions.
